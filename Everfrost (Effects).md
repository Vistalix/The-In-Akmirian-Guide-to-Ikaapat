This page is about the effects for Everfrost. For it itself,, see [[Everfrost]]

Everfrost accumulates in 5 stages:

*[[Exposure]]:* 

[[Exposure]] poses no threats other than a cosmetic cold feeling *upon the skin*. The [[Exposure]] stage is merely when you are in its presence. It is best if everfrost does not get past this stage. 

The feeling of initial [[Exposure]] is often described as having a thin layer of never-melting snow laid upon you.

*[[Frostnip]]:* 

This stage can be identified using the same techniques as identifying the first stages of [[Frostbite]]. As an extension of [[Exposure]], it can also be described as when the snowy feeling begins to go deeper than the dermis. Treatment is not difficult; simply extend your next stay in a warm room, and it should subside on its own.

*[[Enervation]]:*

Negative effects can truly begin to be noticed at this stage. While afflicted with [[Enervation]], one makes a DC 10 CON save for every hour they remain in the cold. A failure results in gaining a stage of [[Exhaustion]].
To identify if you've reached this stage, it is often accompanied by: a slowed or accelerated heartbeat; chills that reach bone; difficulty breathing; and for magic users, instability in casting. From here on out, seek medical attention as soon as possible, as self-identifying is much more difficult if not impossible without specialized equipment.

*[[Frostgnaw]]:*

When [[Frostgnaw]] sets in, know you are one step away from truly permanent damage. Like [[Frostbite]], tissue death at this stage is common (Author's note: NOT FUN!). Though recoverable, it requires specialized methods of removing what can best be described only as a mark left by the Everfrost before recovery can actually begin.

*[[Syphoglacia]]*:

[[Syphoglacia]] is the stage at which [[Everfrost]]'s effects become entirely irreversable. It syphons the very life from whoever was afflicted by it, degrading their very being. Knowledge of this stage is little known, because studies on it were deemed "too unethical, even for those on death row." To my own knowledge from word of mouth, it is most akin to having your body atrophy at a speed that makes it feel as though you were becoming a fossil live.
