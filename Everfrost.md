This page is about Everfrost itself. For its detailed effects, see [[Everfrost (Effects)]]

The Everfrost is a magical cold found mostly in [[In-Akmir]], though it is not exclusive to the area. This cold is staved by ordinary methods, but magical methods of warming typically have no effect. As a result, the [[Lumber Industry]] is quite successful in regions that this cold resides in.

Everfrost accumulates in 5 stages:

*[[Exposure]]:* The first stage of Everfrost takes effect as soon as one is exposed to it. While it is not harmful, its effects are good to be aware of so that you can be sure you haven't passed the stage.

*[[Frostnip]]:* Named after the first stage of [[Frostbite]], due to the two having near identical first stages.

*[[Enervation]]:* This stage is where one will begin to notice differences between Everfrost and [[Frostbite]]. If you find yourself at this stage, you should immediately begin seeking a source of nonmagical warmth if possible.

*[[Frostgnaw]]:* [[Frostgnaw]] is where Everfrost and [[Frostbite]] significantly diverge. Consider this stage what [[Frostbite]] wishes it could be.

*[[Syphoglacia]]*: If you reach this stage, it most probably is too late. Even those who recover from [[Syphoglacia]] are reported to have significantly shortened life expectancies, are more prone to injury, and take longer to recover from ailments.

There are three locations in [[In-Akmir]] where the Everfrost does not permeate:

[[The Garden of In-Akmir]], due to an enchantment placed by an expert sorcerer.

The [[Akmir Wastes]], for a reason not yet understood by researchers.

[[The Sanctuary]], a fantastical location from folktales.
