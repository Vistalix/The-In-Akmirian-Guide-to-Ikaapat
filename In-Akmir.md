In-Akmir is a snowy region found in the north of [[Ikaapat]]. It is one of the three [[major regions]].

*A region whose economy is based on the sale of monster parts to other regions. As a result of their climate, fresh plant produce is a rare commodity, leading to most people's diets consisting of lean meats and imported goods. A small pack of raisins in In-Akmir sells for as much as a bunch of grapes in other regions like [[Dalahmat]]*
