The primary religion in [[Ikaapat]] is the [[Original Order]], an order based on the 7 major gods of the world. These gods are:

[[Ortus]], the God of Beginnings
[[Progressus]], the God of Development
[[Animus]], God of the Mind
[[Mori]], the Goddess of Resolutions & Death
[[Iris]], the Goddess of Nature & Purity
[[Caelum]], the God of the Sky
[[Terra]], the God of the Earth

These 7 gods make up the [[Convention]], and are believed to be the "originators" of everything, including other gods, the universe, and themselves. Generally, it is believed all "lesser" gods answer to the [[Conventionals]].

Within most locations, it's possible to find churches dedicated to the [[Convention]]. It's also not difficult to find churches, temples, or shrines dedicated to other gods or goddesses, though they are less common.

In certain regions, some gods' worshippers receive varying levels of respect. For example, while most people follow the [[Convention]] as a general religion, direct followers of [[Mori]] are praised in [[In-Akmir]] as symbols for resolution to suffering. In [[Dalahmat]] however, they aren't treated so friendily, as they tend to be associated with the end of harvest season. While religious following usually is not a reason for most people to shun others, some extremists have been known to commit acts of terror on sacred grounds or harm to individuals.
