Midden is the capital city of [[In-Akmir]], found north of [[Lake Rise]].

It's known across [[Ikaapat]] for its exceptional placement in [[In-Akmir]], making it the only location in the whole region that can reliably sustain a city of its size.

Heading south from Midden, travelers will come across [[Lake Rise]], the city's main source of water, halfway to the city of [[Meer-Shepper]].
